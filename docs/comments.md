# Comments on the text

Comments work like margin comments in Word: they sit on particular words of a meeting transcript, dictation or Speech item.

## Making a comment

1. Open the recording.
2. Select some words in the text with the mouse (on a phone, press and hold a word, then drag the handles).
3. Press **💬 Comment**, which appears by the words.
4. Write the comment and press **Comment** (or Ctrl + Enter).

The words are shaded yellow, with a small 💬 after them. Clicking the 💬 shows the comment in the **Comments** card on the right.

A comment is on the words of one passage (one box of a meeting, one sentence group of a dictation). If you select across two, it keeps to the first. Up to 200 words.

## Replying and resolving

In the **Comments** card each comment shows the words it is on, who wrote it, and any replies.

- **Reply** opens a box under the comment.
- Tick **Resolved** when it has been dealt with. The shading goes from the text, and the comment is tucked away under "Show N resolved". A new reply opens it again.
- **Remove** takes the comment away; only the person who wrote it can.
- Click the quoted words to go to them in the text. The player is moved there, but does not start playing.

## Who can do what

- Anyone who can open the recording can read the comments.
- The owner, the person it is assigned to (also before accepting, to ask a question), administrators and department admins can comment, reply and resolve.
- Once the recording is approved, its comments are locked. They belong to the whole recording, so a reopened version keeps them.
- Deleting the recording deletes its comments.

## When the text changes

Comments follow their words after edits around them. If the words themselves are changed (for example, "Mr Smith" is corrected to "Mrs Jones"), the comment stays in the card with the note "these words have since been changed".

## Being told

The owner, the person it is assigned to and earlier commenters get a bell notice ("Alice commented on …"), and everyone in a conversation is told about a reply. They also get an email unless they untick **By email** for "Someone left a note or a comment on a recording you work on" on the Notifications page.

The lists show 💬 and the number of open comments after a recording's name.

## History

History records each comment, reply, resolve and removal, with the number of characters and a fingerprint, but never the words of the comment.
Comments are not put in exports and are not searched.
