VerbaFlow Widget

Speak into any text box on a website. Click the microphone button beside the box (or press Alt+Shift+D), speak, then check the text and choose Accept.

1. Allow the microphone

The browser asks once: choose Allow (not "Allow this time"). The Widget only listens while the red Listening panel is showing.

2. Connect to VerbaFlow

Test users only work on the computer where VerbaFlow runs for testing.

Good to know

  • Red means listening, amber means check the text, green means it was added.
  • A plain box the Widget filled is locked so it is not changed by accident. Double-click it to change it.
  • To change part of a long text, select it first, then click the microphone button.
  • Correct by voice: say "scratch that" to take away what you said since the last punctuation mark, or "replace Tuesday with Thursday" (then pause) to change a word you said.
  • The Widget never starts in password or payment boxes, or on websites your administrator has switched it off for.
  • Nothing you say is kept: once you Accept or Cancel, the text exists only in the box. VerbaFlow records only the website, the time and how many words.
